Our Story

25 Years of
Craftsmanship

Art de Gypse was founded in 1999 as a small artisan studio in Paris. Over two and a half decades, we have grown into France's leading manufacturer of gypsum stucco and façade decoration.

Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the signature of skilled hands — from classic Baroque cornices to contemporary minimalist panels.
