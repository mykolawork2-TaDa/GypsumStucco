Our Story

25 Years of
Craftsmanship

ErmitageDecor was founded in 1999 as a small artisan studio in Poltava. Over two and a half decades, we have grown into Ukraine's leading manufacturer of gypsum stucco and façade decoration.

Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the signature of skilled hands and the weight of tradition — from classic Baroque cornices to contemporary minimalist panels.
